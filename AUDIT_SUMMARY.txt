🔍 AUDIT FINDINGS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT'S WORKING:
   • Flutter foundation (board system, actions, design tokens)
   • Entity models (session, turn, response, board action)
   • API integration framework
   • Architecture & planning (comprehensive documentation)

WHAT'S BROKEN:
   1. Board rendering (widgets sometimes don't show)
      Root: Fixed pixels, not responsive; no LayoutBuilder
      Impact: Content off-screen on smaller devices
      
   2. No rich media (can't draw graphs, shapes, vectors, molecules)
      Root: Rendering services not implemented
      Impact: Can't teach visual subjects
      
   3. Not step-by-step (shows whole solution at once)
      Root: No TeachingStep model, backend generates all at once
      Impact: Feels like ChatGPT, not a teacher
